# Competitor / Differentiation Review

## Market Positioning

The MVP should be positioned as the AI translation layer between image-first fashion ideation and maker-ready technical logic.

It is not:
- a pure creative image generator like [Raspberry AI](https://www.raspberry.ai/)
- a full 3D garment simulation platform like [CLO](https://www.clo3d.com/en/) or [Browzwear VStitcher](https://browzwear.com/products/v-stitcher)
- an expert pattern CAD suite like [Lectra Modaris](https://www.lectra.com/en/fashion/products/modaris) or [Gerber AccuMark](https://www.gerbertechnology.com/landing-pages/accumark-family/)
- an enterprise PLM like [Centric PLM](https://www.centricsoftware.com/th/fashion-plm/)

The wedge is narrower and clearer: help independent and inexperienced designers turn ambiguous fashion visuals into editable, reviewable, tailor/manufacturer-readable garment logic.

## Named Competitor Notes

### Techpacker

[Techpacker](https://techpacker.com/?lang=en) is one of the clearest adjacent products. Its official docs show strong support for tech pack PDFs, versioning, stages, and export workflows, including [sharing/downloading tech packs](https://helpcenter.techpacker.com/hc/en-us/articles/360002784033-Step-3-Share-or-download-a-techpack-product), [stages](https://helpcenter.techpacker.com/hc/en-us/articles/360013446574-How-to-use-techpack-product-stages), and [library export to CSV/XLSX/PDF](https://helpcenter.techpacker.com/hc/en-us/articles/4983710786835-How-to-import-or-export-library-data).

Gap: Techpacker helps once the user knows what the garment is. The MVP should help users figure that out first.

### Raspberry AI

[Raspberry AI](https://www.raspberry.ai/) is strong in sketch-to-render, design editing, and visual communication. Its product pages emphasize [design visualization](https://www.raspberry.ai/products/raspberry-overview) and [garment/image editing](https://www.raspberry.ai/products/ai-design-editor).

Gap: it is closer to creative acceleration and merchandising visuals than to maker-ready garment decomposition.

### Tailornova

[Tailornova](https://tailornova.com/) is notable because it is web-based, speaks to hobbyists through brands, and offers custom-fit patterns, flat sketches, PDF, and DXF workflows via smart templates and measurement-driven generation.

Gap: its value is template-driven pattern generation. The MVP should focus instead on interpreting non-standard garments that may not fit preset templates and on producing honest technical assumptions where the source image is incomplete.

### CLO / Browzwear / Style3D

[CLO](https://www.clo3d.com/en/), [Browzwear VStitcher](https://browzwear.com/products/v-stitcher), and [Style3D](https://www.style3d.com/products/studiov8.0) all push strong 3D simulation, fit review, and virtual sampling workflows.

Gap: these are powerful downstream systems, but the MVP should avoid claiming simulation-grade accuracy or full 3D garment development. Its job is earlier: clarify, structure, and hand off.

### Lectra Modaris / Gerber AccuMark

[Lectra Modaris](https://www.lectra.com/en/fashion/products/modaris) and [Gerber AccuMark](https://www.gerbertechnology.com/landing-pages/accumark-family/) are the expert tools for pattern creation, grading, and production planning.

Gap: these are destinations for professionals, not on-ramps for inexperienced designers. Competing on CAD depth would drag the MVP into a much harder product category.

### Centric PLM

[Centric PLM](https://www.centricsoftware.com/th/fashion-plm/) covers design, development, sourcing, manufacturing, and product data management in one cloud platform.

Gap: PLM is organizational infrastructure. The MVP should feel more like a guided garment interpreter and technical co-pilot than a system of record.

## Defensible Uniqueness

The defensible position is not "AI for fashion" in general. It is:

1. Image/sketch-to-technical translation for users who are not pattern experts.
2. Clarification-question engine that reduces ambiguity before export.
3. Dual output modes:
   - Designer Mode for plain-language refinement
   - Maker/Tailor Mode for structured technical handoff
4. Detailed Pattern Map that explains likely pieces, seam relationships, symmetry/asymmetry, layers, facings, lining, reversibility, detachable modules, and uncertainty.
5. Honest handling of unusual garments: asymmetric, modular, reversible, exaggerated, sculptural, mixed-material, and incomplete-from-image concepts.

Most adjacent tools either start too early in the creative funnel or too late in the technical funnel. The product can own the missing middle.

## Feature Gaps to Exploit

- Creative AI tools generate attractive concepts but usually stop before construction logic.
- Tech pack tools document decisions but do not reliably generate them from ambiguous visuals.
- CAD and 3D tools require expertise and time before value appears.
- PLM tools coordinate teams but do not teach garment definition.
- Existing tools are better for standard garments than for weird, asymmetric, layered, detachable, or partially hidden concepts.

## Do Not Compete Head-On With X

- Do not compete head-on with [CLO](https://www.clo3d.com/en/), [Browzwear](https://browzwear.com/products/v-stitcher), or [Style3D](https://www.style3d.com/products/studiov8.0) on 3D simulation, drape realism, or fit validation.
- Do not compete head-on with [Lectra Modaris](https://www.lectra.com/en/fashion/products/modaris) or [Gerber AccuMark](https://www.gerbertechnology.com/landing-pages/accumark-family/) on CAD-grade patternmaking, grading, or production-certified outputs.
- Do not compete head-on with [Centric PLM](https://www.centricsoftware.com/th/fashion-plm/) on enterprise workflow breadth, sourcing, or cross-functional system-of-record behavior.
- Do not compete head-on with [Raspberry AI](https://www.raspberry.ai/) on photorealistic creative generation, campaign assets, or visual merchandising.
- Do not compete head-on with [Techpacker](https://techpacker.com/?lang=en) on mature team workflow breadth for brands that already know how to write tech packs.

## Recommended Positioning Statement

An AI-first garment interpretation tool for independent designers and emerging brands that turns fashion images and sketches into editable, maker-ready technical logic through clarification, dual designer/maker workflows, detailed pattern mapping, and PDF/Excel export.
