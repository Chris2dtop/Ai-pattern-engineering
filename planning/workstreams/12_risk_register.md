# Risk Register

This register focuses on the MVP risk areas most likely to damage trust, margin, or launch readiness. The main control themes are: do not overclaim production readiness, keep uncertainty visible, and enforce strict AI cost budgets.

| Category | Risk | Severity | Likelihood | Trigger | Mitigation | Owner / Phase |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Product | Users interpret "maker-ready" exports as production-certified patterns or factory-ready instructions. | High | High | Marketing copy, UI labels, or exports imply exact pattern accuracy or manufacturing certification. | Use explicit "reviewable technical draft" language everywhere; label inferred fields; add export warnings and professional-review requirement for high-risk garments; keep CAD/DXF/grading claims out of MVP. | Product + Content / Pre-launch and ongoing |
| Product | The product tries to cover CAD, 3D simulation, grading, and sourcing too early, weakening the core workflow. | High | Medium | Roadmap or launch scope expands beyond concept-to-technical-package translation. | Hold MVP scope to upload, clarification, technical package, pattern map, and export; treat CAD/3D as later integrations, not hidden promises. | Product / Planning |
| Technical | AI jobs or exports fail mid-flow, causing lost work or duplicate charges. | High | Medium | Queue retries create duplicate outputs, exports fail after long waits, or regenerated content overwrites edits. | Use idempotent jobs, saved project revisions, partial section saves, last-successful export retention, and no-charge retries for failed generations/exports. | Engineering / Build |
| Technical | Schema drift or weak validation lets contradictory POM, BOM, pattern map, and construction notes reach export. | High | Medium | A package exports with inconsistent closures, missing pieces, or unresolved but unlabeled assumptions. | Enforce schema and consistency validation after each major step; block or warn on unresolved critical contradictions; preserve audit trail of AI output vs user edits. | Engineering + AI / Build |
| AI Quality | The model invents hidden construction details for asymmetric, reversible, detachable, or mixed-material garments. | High | High | Output states hidden seams, linings, supports, or attachment methods as facts from a limited image. | Require visible/user_provided/inferred basis on generated fields; route low-confidence areas to clarification; raise review warnings for high-impact inferred details; avoid one-shot generation. | AI / Build and tuning |
| AI Quality | Confidence labels become decorative and do not actually change UX or export behavior. | High | Medium | Low-confidence fields appear in exports without friction, or users skip unresolved ambiguity. | Tie confidence thresholds to behavior: ask clarifications, block critical exports, or require explicit approval when confidence is below threshold. | Product + AI / Build |
| Legal / Liability | Users rely on outputs for production, then claim losses from defects, fit issues, or unsafe construction assumptions. | High | Medium | A failed sample or production run is attributed to the platform's technical guidance. | Clear Terms and in-product disclaimers; visible review-required warnings; no fit guarantee, certification, or production-readiness claims; retain audit logs of assumptions and user approvals. | Founder + Legal / Pre-launch |
| Legal / Liability | Marketing or sales copy overstates capability versus what the MVP can actually validate. | High | Medium | Landing copy promises precise patterns, factory-ready outputs, or reduced need for expert review. | Review all copy against MVP non-scope; prefer conservative language such as "technical interpretation" and "maker-readable draft"; avoid testimonial wording that implies guaranteed outcomes. | Founder + Marketing / Pre-launch |
| Cost | AI cost per project rises above plan due to repeated full-pack regenerations, Pro overuse, or large image inputs. | High | High | Average project cost exceeds budget guardrails or margin assumptions. | Enforce per-job and per-project budget caps; cache image analysis and approved state; regenerate only changed sections; default to Gemini Flash/Lite; gate Pro behind validation failure or paid review. | Engineering + Finance / Build and launch |
| Cost | Free users or trial users consume disproportionate multimodal compute without converting. | Medium | High | Trial cohorts upload many assets, regenerate heavily, or abandon before payment. | Tight free-credit limits, image count/size limits, queued processing, and restricted premium retries on free plans; monitor trial-to-paid and cost per activated user. | Product + Finance / Launch |
| UX / Trust | Designers feel the system is confidently wrong, while makers still find exports too ambiguous to use. | High | Medium | Pilot users report that output sounds polished but does not reduce back-and-forth. | Optimize for conservative clarity over fluent prose; expose unresolved questions early; keep Designer and Maker modes aligned; test with both designers and makers before launch. | Product + UX Research / Pilot |
| UX / Trust | Pattern Map visuals imply exact geometry or precision that the system does not truly have. | High | Medium | Diagrams look like production pattern files even when based on inference. | Use diagram styles that show relationships, not exact outlines; display review badges on pieces and joins; remove CAD-like controls and terminology from MVP. | Product + Design / Build |
| Data / Privacy | Uploaded garment images, project notes, or exports are exposed through weak storage or URL handling. | High | Medium | Private assets are publicly reachable or shared beyond the owning user/org. | Private-by-default storage, signed URLs, RLS on project tables, server-side AI access only, and environment-specific storage controls. | Engineering / Build |
| Data / Privacy | More user or payment data than necessary is sent to model providers or logs. | High | Medium | Prompts include billing/contact details or logs retain sensitive payloads too broadly. | Minimize provider payloads to garment-relevant context only; separate payment data from AI flows; redact logs where possible; keep provider keys server-side only. | Engineering + Security / Build |
| Go-to-Market | The positioning blurs into Techpacker, Tailornova, or 3D/CAD tools, making the wedge hard to understand. | Medium | Medium | Prospects ask whether the product is a PLM, pattern CAD tool, or image generator. | Anchor messaging on "image/sketch to editable technical interpretation"; emphasize clarification engine, dual designer/maker modes, and honest pattern mapping for unusual garments. | Founder + Marketing / Pre-launch |
| Go-to-Market | Early users expect standard-garment speed, but the real strength is unusual garments with ambiguity. | Medium | Medium | Acquisition channels bring users whose needs are already solved by templates or existing tech pack tools. | Target independent designers, students, emerging brands, and makers dealing with unclear or complex concepts; use examples featuring asymmetric, reversible, detachable, or sculptural garments. | Founder + Marketing / Launch |

## Watch Metrics

- Export packages with unresolved high-risk flags
- Average AI cost per successful paid project
- Pro-tier fallback rate
- Clarification completion rate
- Pilot feedback on "clearer than image alone"
- Percentage of exports requiring maker follow-up on hidden construction details
- Support tickets or complaints tied to overclaim / production-readiness confusion
